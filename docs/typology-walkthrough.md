# Walkthrough: current colour and style proposals

Snapshot for discussion, 1 November 2026. These are design proposals, not validated diagnoses or a finished universal classification of people. This packet contains no personal case material. The underlying documents contain source attribution, examples and important limits.

## Colour: 31 Opal templates

The [atlas data](../data/colour-atlas-31.json) is the full source for names, factor coordinates, illustrative swatches, aliases and references. Temperature (cool–neutral–warm), value (light–medium–deep) and chroma (muted–moderate–vivid) are continuous, uncertain factors; neutral is a value and unknown is not neutral. Natural contrast is an additional observation, not a fourth axis. The 31 are twelve conventional seasonal classes **included within** the system plus 19 new proposals, formed from 27 coarse cells with four splits. Multiple names at one coarse coordinate represent split cells, not duplicates to discard. Swatches are illustrative and not draping-validated.

The atlas retains finer coordinates and seasonal tilt, so this table is only an orientation. A palette preference does not establish undertone; keep suitability and personal likes separate. Each palette should provide usable light and dark neutrals and multiple hue families.

## Adult silhouette and impression: separate menus

[Proposed names and crosswalk](evals/style-type-names-proposal.md) contains the full six silhouettes and four impression overlays, their garment comparisons, a historical 13-label crosswalk, a seven-essence source crosswalk and limitations. These names describe outfit hypotheses, not body types, and do not form a mandatory 6×4 grid:

- **Silhouette:** Spire (extended directional line), Arcade (extended curves and transitions), Pavilion (supported upper construction and ease), Atrium (affirmed moderation), Mosaic (compact segments and interruption), Cameo (compact rounded line).
- **Visual impression:** Chisel (precise, sparse, directional), Lilt (rhythmic, lightly playful), Filigree (fine curved ornament), Haze (diffuse, flowing, fine surface).

Colour's Mosaic and silhouette's Mosaic are independent names in different menus. A person can explore, mix or reject the names; regional descriptions may be more useful. The menus are not exhaustive, and no percentages, diagnostic thresholds or personality claims follow from them.

## Factor map and evidence boundaries

[Style factor research](evals/style-factor-research.md) maps published questions and garment descriptions to candidate observations, missing combinations and a small qualitative visual probe. Preserve region, view and uncertainty while distinguishing length, breadth, edge, slope, scale and contour. Separate visible geometry from garment construction, material and composition, visual impression, and the person's taste and practical needs. Height, perceived elongation and clothing-created continuity are distinct. Broad and sharp, or fine and full, can coexist. Opposing features do not automatically average into moderation. Do not infer hidden anatomy, personality, exact measurements or fibre content from a photograph.

The source crosswalks attribute their own authors' frameworks; they are not conversion rules or evidence of scientific validity. The visual probe did not validate typing or establish the effect of any garment change. Consult the [colour data sources](../data/colour-atlas-31.json), [factor source map](evals/style-factor-research.md) and [naming proposal](evals/style-type-names-proposal.md) for links and qualifications.

## Suggested ChatGPT walkthrough

Give ChatGPT this page and the three linked artifacts, or paste their contents if it cannot access the repository. Ask it to explain the systems and their source relationships, walk through the 31 entries and ten proposed names, identify overlap or missing combinations, and suggest useful comparisons. Have it label source claims versus this project's interpretations, uncertainties and open choices. Ask it to preserve continuous factors, local mixtures, unknowns and separate taste from harmony; it should not assign a person a type or treat labels as validated facts. For an actual consultation, compare appealing coherent outfits and ask separately about visual effect, affinity, practicality and rendering fidelity. Both, neither and uncertain are valid. A rendering error is not evidence about someone's body.

The [adult planning prompt](../prompts/style/plan.md) provides the fuller operational guidance and domain-specific continuations. Adult menswear and other adult garment contexts use distinct construction guidance; child essence is separate and does not use adult body typing. The user decides when exploration is satisfactory.
