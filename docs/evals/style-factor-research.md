# Style factors: source map and native visual probe

Research/design proposal, 1 November 2026. These are candidate observations and
comparisons, **not approved axes, a validated typing instrument or deployed code**.
The product outcome is a coherent, enjoyable wardrobe language that can combine
features a type label separates. Its owner judges its appeal. No paid inference,
image generation or celebrity editing is part of this probe.

## What the sources establish

Sources were selected from the parallel research lanes, then the original pages
below were inspected. A site's own quiz is primary evidence of **its questions**,
not proof of a person's type or the scientific validity of that system. Private
design notes 02/18 informed evidence/domain boundaries; no personal case material
is reproduced.

- Kibbe's [publisher](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/723284/david-kibbes-power-of-style-by-david-kibbe/)
  describes the January 2025 book as updating Image Identity and colour systems.
  An independent quiz's historical categories or formula cannot silently stand
  in for his current method.
- Kitchener's [own account](https://www.pscjohnkitchener.com/meet-john-kitchener)
  describes seven harmonies and explicitly allows four or more, even all seven.
  A three-essence cap is therefore inappropriate as a Kitchener rule. This is
  attribution of his framework, not empirical validation.
- [Body Shape Test](https://bodyshapetest.org/kibbe/) explicitly calls its questions,
  height bins, reference patterns and equal dimension weights editorial and
  unvalidated. It usefully preserves sharp versus blunt yang and mixed extremes
  versus uniform moderation, but not feature location within each dimension.
- [My True Style](https://www.mytruestyle.com/kitchener-style-essence-quiz/) is a
  noisy adaptation: its Gamine result heading contains Romantic prose/examples,
  and its Romantic heading contains Gamine prose/examples. Its eye question
  repeats an answer; direct HTML inspection confirms distinct controls with
  different Gamine/Classic metadata, not a text-extraction duplicate. The final
  score calculation was not executed. Its result text is unsuitable as a target.

## Finite source → factor → gap map

The last two columns are **our proposed decompositions and counterexamples**.
They are not facts asserted by the source. Preserve the region and original
answer alongside any interpretation; unknown does not mean moderate.

| Source / coupled question or rule | Candidate observations kept apart | Combination otherwise obscured |
|---|---|---|
| [Face Shape Lab](https://faceshapelab.com/kibbe-body-type-test/), shoulders: narrow/sharp, broad/blunt, small/sharp, sloped/narrow/soft alternatives | Relative breadth; corner definition; slope; local scale | Broad + sharp; narrow + blunt; broad + sloping |
| Same quiz, limbs: length bundled with breadth or delicacy | Leg/torso and arm/torso relationships; apparent breadth separately | Long limbs + delicate extremities; different arm/leg relationships |
| Same quiz, hands/feet and overall bone descriptions | Local length, breadth, prominence, contour; keep hands and feet distinct | Broad shoulders + fine wrists/hands, without averaging them away |
| Same quiz, eyes/lips and facial bones | Opening/outline, feature size relative to face, fullness, projection; source photograph/crop | Small-scale full lips; angular jaw + rounded cheeks |
| [Body Shape Test](https://bodyshapetest.org/kibbe/), grouped letter distributions | Region/feature assignment of every observation as well as distribution | Swapping broad shoulders and sharp joints cannot be treated as the same styling problem |
| Same source, separate slight/fine and rounded/full answers | Apparent fineness/scale versus contour fullness | Fine bones + lush contours; compactness without lushness |
| [Concept Wardrobe](https://theconceptwardrobe.com/7-style-essences/complete-style-essences-guides), Dramatic versus Natural clothing | Line length; angularity; garment ease; structural stiffness | Long + angular + lightweight; broad + fluid + polished |
| Same guide, Gamine versus Ingenue | Compactness; edge shape; ornament size; ornament density | Compact + rounded + sparse; compact + angular + restrained |
| Same guide, Romantic versus Angelic | Curvature; line length; surface fineness; fluidity/transparency | Elongated curves + substantial opaque cloth; lush shapes + sparse detail |
| Same guide, colour and aesthetic language accompany shape | Palette contrast/value/chroma separate from construction and intended mood | Playfulness in muted colours; strong geometry in light colours |
| [My True Style](https://www.mytruestyle.com/kitchener-style-essence-quiz/), face/eyes/body choices mingle geometry and social adjectives | Visible feature description versus author's aesthetic impression | Rounded eyes + an angular composition; breadth without a claim of friendliness |
| Same quiz, suit response/first impression/voice questions | User-reported garment experience; desired expression; optional communication context | A loved sharp jacket need not prove bodily angularity, temperament or an essence |

This is richer than a single yin–yang coordinate without requiring a universal
ontology. Sharpness and breadth are different observations; delicacy and fullness
are different observations; mixed extremes retain their locations. “Balanced”
requires affirmative evidence of moderation, not the arithmetic cancellation of
opposites. A dominant label can remain useful shorthand after these distinctions
survive, rather than deciding them in advance.

## Four kinds of information, four uses

1. **Visible geometry:** region-specific, view-specific descriptions with unknowns.
   A garment's outline does not reveal exact underlying anatomy or measurements.
2. **Construction and cloth:** shoulder support, seams, ease, openings, waist/hem,
   material behaviour, surface and detail. Appearance is not verified fibre content.
3. **Aesthetic impression:** tentative whole-look language such as clean, ornate,
   playful or flowing. Essence can be a useful holistic hypothesis; it is neither
   a personality measurement nor a required sum of feature scores.
4. **Taste and practicality:** the person's actual likes, dislikes, comfort,
   categories, occasions and constraints. A critic's harmony judgement cannot
   overwrite these. An attractive photograph cannot establish comfort or fit.

Factorisation alone does not compose a good look. Keep transitions and
interactions: a crisp shoulder can coexist with a fluid lower garment; fine
jewellery can repeat a local feature without dictating the entire silhouette.
Generate appealing coherent alternatives, then learn which interaction matters.

## Compact observation prompt to try

This is an experimental block for the existing planning prompt, not a fourth
production template, a new schema or an exhaustive intake questionnaire.

> Observe only the supplied adult reference images and explicit clothing domain.
> First list visible garment/body-region evidence with image/view references and
> uncertainty. Keep breadth, edge, slope, scale, length and contour separate where
> the image supports them; leave hidden or ambiguous regions unknown. Do not
> manufacture a complete factor vector. Keep exact user reports, observations,
> styling hypotheses, aesthetic impressions and taste in separate records.
> Preserve local mixtures and contradictions; never collapse them into a global
> yin–yang average. Give one tentative holistic reading after the observations.
> Propose the requested number of appealing coherent looks and explain the specific garment–region
> interactions being tested. Keep loved details unless explicitly changing them.
> A celebrity label or caption is a source claim, not visible evidence. Report
> recognition/caption contamination. Do not infer personality or exact measures.
> Seek feedback separately on visual effect, affinity, practicality and rendering
> fidelity; both, neither and uncertain are valid. A rendering error updates the
> rendering instructions, not the person's body or taste.

Use **one** domain continuation, selected by the user's clothing choice:

- **Menswear construction:** reason about shoulder support/slope, sleevehead and
  armhole, collar opening, lapel/gorge, chest ease, button stance/coat length,
  trouser rise/seat/thigh/leg/break. Choose relevant variables, not mandatory suits
  or ties. Do not turn women's regional line heuristics into a men's body quiz.
- **Other adult garments:** reason about regional neckline/shoulder/torso/waist/
  hip/limb lines, volume and length, transitions, support and drape. Preserve
  combinations of tailoring and softness. Do not infer garment domain from gender.

## Actual native visual trial

Two public adult event portraits in one existing typing-article comparison were
viewed locally on 1 November. The checklist reading was made by this document's
author; a fresh native child received only the image and a holistic garment task,
without this map, names, source URL or requested type. A practical stylist reading
will be reconciled below. This is a small qualitative probe, not an accuracy test.

The [Concept Wardrobe Dramatic article](https://theconceptwardrobe.com/7-style-essences/the-dramatic-style-essence-a-comprehensive-guide-the-concept-wardrobe)
contains the [paired event image](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5eca30fd2b50b671e2107b06/64d68360f504d418ad2be8fe_Dramatic%20Style%20Essence%20Test.webp).
The author recognised Charlize Theron at left and Eva Longoria at right. Later
event reporting corroborated the identities and ensembles: [Theron at the April
2019 Long Shot premiere](https://www.redcarpet-fashionawards.com/2019/05/01/charlize-theron-debuts-her-new-bangs-at-the-long-shot-new-york-premiere/)
and [Longoria at the October 2021 ELLE event](https://www.journaldesfemmes.fr/mode/looks-de-star/2782431-ces-looks-d-eva-longoria-pourraient-tous-etre-portes-par-gabrielle-solis/2782447-eva-longoria-le-19-octobre-2021).
Those separately linked photos were also viewed; they show the same ensembles,
with a different pose in Theron's case. Embedded yes/no essence captions
are visible, so **label blinding failed** even though no target was put in the
child's brief. The event reports credit Getty/Vogue and Rob Latour/Shutterstock/
Sipa respectively; the typing collage itself did not establish its full rights
chain. Editing permission was not established. Only links and observation prose belong in Git; local
viewing caches are not evaluation redistributables.

No measured proportions, taste or fit verdict. |

The article's categorical caption cannot resolve either hypothesis. The photos
change person, outfit cut, surface, pose, background and lighting simultaneously.
A comparison of the two is useful for noticing variables, not estimating the
causal effect of changing one. The labels are attributed aesthetic judgements,
not secret answers the model should recover.

### Independent reading and reconciliation

The fresh child actually viewed this same image. It disclosed the embedded
caption leakage. Its holistic reading found a contained, relatively uninterrupted
left silhouette with restrained volume and a small texture accent; at right it
noticed a flowing, layered composition, central fastening and conspicuous
reflections. It proposed a longer straight jacket at left, and a comparable matte
jacket at right. These are independent proposal differences, not observed garment
responses. It inferred no type, personality, taste or hidden anatomy.

The root stylist actually viewed the image too, **after seeing this author's
proposed controls**, so its critique is not an independent hypothesis source.
It judged both existing looks coherent event tailoring, cautioned against making
the right portrait a deliberately poor control, and refined the experiments:
retain heel, rise, width, stance and camera when testing trouser length; retain
pattern, thickness, ease and drape when testing surface. Real fabric substitutions
often change flow/weight too. Preserve the belt during a surface test; investigate
belt scale/placement separately only if that is a relevant wearer concern.

| Issue | Agreement / difference | Consequence and confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Visible clothing differences | All readings distinguish contained dark tailoring at left from reflective, belted, broader cloth at right. | Strong agreement about this image's garment presentation; no evidence of type accuracy. |
| Left look's continuity | Holistic reading calls it relatively uninterrupted; checklist emphasises ankle/neckline breaks. | Compatible levels of description, not a disagreement to vote away. Compare jacket length **or** trouser length first, not both; let the wearer's retained detail/intended impression choose. |
| Right surface emphasis | Independent holistic reader also proposes matte versus reflective cloth. | A useful convergent hypothesis; causal confidence remains low until drape/weight and lighting are controlled. |
| Actual success | No alternative was rendered or worn and no wearer responded. | No claim that either change improves the look. Taste, comfort and motion remain unknown. |
| Published labels | Captions favour left over right for this author's Dramatic category. | Captions were visible before judgement, so a held-out comparison is impossible here. Record leakage; do not report classification agreement. |

This trial demonstrates that the checklist preserves locations while the
holistic method contributes composition-level questions. It does not show one
method is more accurate. The practical critique improves control design without
converting agreement into proof. Before a live comparison, ask which detail the
person wants to retain and what impression they want the outfit to create.

### Clean-reference follow-up: labels withheld from one fresh reader

To improve the failed caption blinding, a **second fresh** native child viewed
only the two direct event-report photos, cached locally under neutral filenames.
It received no checklist, names, URLs, published labels or earlier responses.
These show the same two cases/ensembles; Theron's stance differs from the typing
collage, so this is a follow-up, not a same-pixel replication. The checklist
author and practical critic had already seen labels; the follow-up does not erase
that contamination. The fresh reader recognised the public faces and disclosed
that identity blinding also failed. Type labels were absent from its inputs.

Its actual observations add a useful whole-composition reading: at left, the
neckline, lapels and pointed shoes repeat directional edges; exposed neckline/
ankles provide space around the dark suit; lace and red lip are local accents.
It also noticed that backdrop/carpet red contributes to photographic coherence,
which cannot be credited to the clothes alone. At right, overlapping fronts lead
into the belt; broad reflections and long folds create movement, with bright
earrings/buckle as smaller accents. Smooth hair repeats the flowing surface.

Its hypotheses differ from the initial checklist: edge repetition may explain
the left composition, and the right belt may organise fabric fullness rather
than merely emphasise the waist. It proposed the same cut in matte versus satin
for **both** cases. The practical critique still applies: such a material swap
is confounded unless weight/drape remain comparable. No variants were made.

Only after this reader returned did this document compare its reading with the
typing article's left-positive/right-negative Dramatic captions. The reader
described both compositions coherently and supplied no type verdict. There is
therefore no classification match to score. The proposed mechanisms—edge
repetition, reflective movement, belt organisation—are more useful experimental
questions than treating the right outfit as a failure. Agreement about reflection
is robust across these readings; its desirability for either wearer is unknown.

## What this increment can support

The map exposes concrete quiz blind spots and yields controllable clothing
comparisons. It does not establish new natural kinds, factor independence,
measurement reliability, an essence percentage or a type accuracy score.
Next useful evidence is a matched on-person comparison with stable pose/light,
checking actual rendering fidelity before interpreting feedback. Test harmony
and taste separately; retain both/neither/uncertain. Current source photographs
cannot validate either on-user generation or eventual consultation satisfaction.
