# Stage 2 integration checkpoint — 1 November

**Intent:** An expert stylist uses the person's evidence and preferences to produce on-person outfit sheets, explores again after rejection, preserves likes, and continues until the person explicitly approves, pauses, or exhausts their own allowance. Editorial follows approval. Wardrobe complementarity remains unevaluated without inventory.

**Demonstrated so far:** A provisional responsive intake is visible with a synthetic transport fixture. A browser fixture confirms that delayed saves retain later edits and a revision conflict allows the complete draft to be reapplied. Disposable PostgreSQL checks cover reservations, a simultaneous pool-cap race, approval/input hashes, deletion and generated-upload leases; provider transport is mocked. A fictional three-outfit imagegen experiment demonstrates a possible sheet layout, not a running app or evidence of personal suitability. The production build succeeds.

**Missing or different:** Paid generation and editorial are hard-disabled; no production migration or paid API request has occurred. The disabled planner now accepts the reference photo, but photo-informed body-line or essence judgement remains untested. The sheet, reject-all followed by fresh alternatives, preservation of likes through several rounds, budget pause, real-person likeness and actual editorial remain undemonstrated in the app. The owner must accept the qualitative prompt before paid API tests. Costs, especially defensible vision and image-input ceilings, and deletion/upload integration also require verification before dispatch. The imagegen experiment has no known client taste and its outfit choices are not stylistic validation.

**Live fallback:** Stage 1 remains available at https://personal-stylist-mauve.vercel.app and has not been replaced by this checkpoint. The owner judges visual appeal and product sufficiency. The pending implementation and prompt/evaluation work must be reviewed against the intent above rather than declared complete from a build or single revision.

**Consultation conversation:** The user wants the feedback loop to actively ask why and elicit specifics, particularly after vague dislike or rejecting every option; the user can also ask why, and loved details must survive clarification before another costly generation. Voice belongs in this loop but is stretch; generous multiline text is the immediate alternative. The provisional form can collect detailed thoughts and questions, but does not yet answer questions or conduct a back-and-forth clarification: that remains required, not a completed conversation. Domain workflow is chosen explicitly for requested clothing, never inferred gender; mixed/custom cases need useful clarification, not a silent default.
