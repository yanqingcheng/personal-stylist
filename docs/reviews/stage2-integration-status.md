# Stage 2 integration checkpoint — 1 November

**Intent:** An expert stylist uses the person's evidence and preferences to produce on-person outfit sheets, explores again after rejection, preserves likes, and continues until the person explicitly approves, pauses, or exhausts their own allowance. Editorial follows approval. Wardrobe complementarity remains unevaluated without inventory.

**Demonstrated so far:** A provisional responsive intake is visible with a synthetic transport fixture. A browser fixture confirms that delayed saves retain later edits and a revision conflict allows the complete draft to be reapplied. Disposable PostgreSQL checks cover reservations, a simultaneous pool-cap race, approval/input hashes, deletion and generated-upload leases; provider transport is mocked. A fictional three-outfit imagegen experiment demonstrates a possible sheet layout, not a running app or evidence of personal suitability. The production build succeeds.

**Missing or different:** Paid generation and editorial are hard-disabled; no production migration or paid API request has occurred. The current text planner does not inspect the reference photo, so it cannot yet provide photo-informed body-line or essence judgement. The sheet, reject-all followed by fresh alternatives, preservation of likes through several rounds, budget pause, real-person likeness and actual editorial remain undemonstrated in the app. Costs, especially a defensible image-input ceiling, and deletion/upload integration require further verification before dispatch. The imagegen experiment has no known client taste and its outfit choices are not stylistic validation.

**Live fallback:** Stage 1 remains available at https://personal-stylist-mauve.vercel.app and has not been replaced by this checkpoint. The owner judges visual appeal and product sufficiency. The pending implementation and prompt/evaluation work must be reviewed against the intent above rather than declared complete from a build or single revision.
